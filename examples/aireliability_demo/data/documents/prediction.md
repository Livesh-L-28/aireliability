# Predictive Reliability & Risk Forecasting

Phase 42 implements empirical forecasting of AI system reliability, failure probabilities, and regression risks.
Rather than reacting after production outages occur, prediction identifies degrading trends in advance.

## Forecasting Capabilities
- **Time-Series Analysis**: Forecasts reliability scores across short-term, medium-term, and long-term horizons.
- **Confidence Intervals**: Computes rigorous upper and lower bounds with sample-size uncertainty penalties.
- **Failure Risk Estimation**: Calculates the probability of impending SLA violations or sudden regression spikes.
- **Trend Diagnostics**: Classifies performance into STABLE, IMPROVING, DEGRADING, or VOLATILE regimes.
